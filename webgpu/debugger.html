About

SDP bound analysis (Angeris 2022) applied to Continuous Thought Machines. Each tick is one thinking step. The gap measures how far the model's activations are from the SDP-optimal. Forward correction nudges activations toward optimal at each tick — closing the gap without backpropagation.
